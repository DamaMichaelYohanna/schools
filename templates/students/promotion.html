How End-of-Session Promotion & Fees Work:

Promoted: Student advances to the next class arm (e.g. JSS 1A → JSS 2A).
Repeating: Student is retained in their current class level. All their past scores and report cards remain permanently archived. Because a new academic session will commence, the repeating student will automatically be billed fresh fees for that class level in the new session.
Graduated: For final-year SSS 3 students who completed secondary education. They become alumni and are no longer billed.
